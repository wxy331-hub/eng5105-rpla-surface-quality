## Technical relevance of Plan A

Raspberry Pi's official product page states that the IMX296-based Global Shutter Camera captures all pixels simultaneously, supports C/CS-mount lenses, and is intended for rapid motion and machine-vision applications. These capabilities justify Plan A's technical strength in the comparison. They do not override the availability, incremental-cost, and delivery gates for this semester project.
