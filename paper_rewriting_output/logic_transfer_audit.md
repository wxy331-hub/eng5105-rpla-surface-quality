## Controlled limitations

The final reports do not invent the three inputs that were not supplied: dated stakeholder consultation outcomes, the exact campus/Traditional Owner group needed for a more localised Acknowledgement of Country, and team names/headshots. These omissions limit the attainable rubric band until genuine information is added, but preserving them is the only defensible transfer of the available evidence.
