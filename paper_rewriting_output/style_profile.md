# Style Profile

| Style Dimension | Target Venue Expectation | Exemplar Pattern | Applied To This Report |
|---|---|---|---|
| Controlling argument | A client-facing proposal must move from need to justified recommendation, not merely describe components. | Strong engineering reports state the decision early and then earn it through requirements, trade studies, calculations, risks, and verification. | The executive summary names Plan B and its decisive feasibility logic; later sections trace each requirement through architecture, specification, risk, and planned verification. |
| Rubric visibility | Every required category must be easy for a marker to locate, with greatest depth in functional details and project planning. | Layer the document for decision-makers and technical reviewers; use headings and cross-references as navigation. | Mirror the rubric's content order while allocating the greatest analytical depth to the 25% functional section and 20% plan/recommendation section. |
| Concept selection | At least two differentiated concepts, benefits, limitations, requirement fit, and an evidence-backed choice. | Apply non-negotiable feasibility gates before weighted preference scoring. Record criteria, sources, assumptions, and limitations. | Compare Plan A and Plan B, then reject Plan A on unavailable hardware, Plan-A-specific incremental cost, lead time, and semester deliverability. Technically compare only after those gates are explicit. |
| Requirements | Needs should become measurable specifications and remain traceable to system components and verification. | Use a compact requirement-to-feature-to-evidence matrix with provenance and status. | Label unconfirmed numerical targets `PROPOSED_REQUIREMENT`; provide verification methods and consultation questions without inventing stakeholder approval. |
| Evidence language | Highest-quality work uses accurate primary sources and distinguishes measured results from proposals or assumptions. | Record source, conditions, sample basis, status, and limitations beside each claim. | Preserve `LIVE_INTEGRATION_ONLY`, `OFFLINE_SYNTHETIC_ONLY`, and `PENDING_REAL_MATERIAL`; state what each result proves and does not prove. |
| Functional explanation | A detailed block diagram must show sensors, processing, communication, API, UI, power, and outputs; each block must link back to needs. | Introduce each figure before it appears and interpret its decision significance afterward. Label arrows, interfaces, protocols, domains, and boundaries. | Explain ESP32S3 acquisition, Wi-Fi MJPEG, Pi service/API/database/GUI, STM32 timing/safe I/O, auxiliary sensing, power domains, and fault states in one traceable chain. |
| Quantitative detail | Specifications, power budget, BOM costs, and validation targets must be calculated or sourced. | Tables carry repeated fields; prose explains assumptions, trade-offs, and consequences. | Use transparent calculation bases and margins. Mark unverified price estimates or supplier quotes as pending and never charge shared components against Plan A. |
| Project plan | Milestones, dependencies, risks, planning strategy, and evidence outputs must show how the proposal becomes a validated prototype. | Make verification deliverables and decision gates visible, not just weekly activity lists. | Treat Week 5 Thursday material arrival as a hard dependency; schedule calibration and real-rPLA performance testing only after receipt. |
| Tone and humanisation | Professional, concise, technically precise, and free from repetitive template prose. | Use concrete subjects/verbs, calibrated claims, varied paragraph moves, and short/long sentence contrast. | Apply medium PaperSpine humanisation separately in English and Chinese, varying sentence and paragraph structure without changing facts, numbers, terms, or citations. |
| Conclusion | Recommendations must synthesize requirement fit, trade-offs, unresolved evidence, and next actions. | Close the decision loop instead of repeating section summaries. | Reaffirm why Plan B is implementable now, name the conditions under which its performance will be accepted, and preserve limitations until evidence exists. |
